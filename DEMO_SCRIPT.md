## 💡 Pro Recording & Presentation Tips for the Video

| Element | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Microphone & Audio** | Use a clear condenser or lapel mic. Ensure zero background noise. Keep voice enthusiastic and authoritative. |
| **Screen Resolution** | Record in **1080p or 4K at 60 FPS** (using OBS Studio). Ensure browser is in full-screen (`F11`). |
| **Mouse Movements** | Smooth, deliberate cursor movements. Avoid rapid frantic clicking. Hover over tooltips to show the depth. |
| **Theme & Contrast** | Dark theme looks incredible on video. Ensure browser zoom is at 100% or 90% so all cards and Deck.gl map fit comfortably. |
| **Pacing** | Practice along with this script once before recording. It fits effortlessly into 9 minutes and 15 seconds. |
